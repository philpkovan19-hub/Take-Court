HOW IT WORKS

1

FILE!

Post your take (up to 512 chars) with a small filing fee. Set the trial length (1 hour to 30 days).

2

STAKE!

Anyone can stake BOT to Crown (agree) or Bonk (disagree). Bigger stakes count for more. You can't switch sides.

3

VERDICT!

When time's up, anyone can trigger adjudication. Whichever pool is bigger wins. Tie = full refund for all.

4

CASH IN!

Winners claim: get your stake back + a proportional slice of the losing pool (minus 3% platform fee). Losers get nothing.
